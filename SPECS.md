# Skill specs (locked with the founder, Oct 29 2026)

Ten skills. All follow CONVENTIONS.md. Shopify only for store data. Setup is per skill (no shared profile).

## 1. catalog-photoshoot
Turn raw product photos (garment on a mannequin, on a person, flat lay, or any non-clothing product) into consistent catalog images with an AI model or as studio/lifestyle product shots. Merchant picks the image provider at setup (Magnific or Higgsfield, whichever connector they have). Setup: provider + model, the model's look (gender, age range, body type, skin tone, hair, footwear default, modesty level including hijab option), how the model face is fixed (generate 4 candidates and pick, or upload a reference face), store language, credit limit per run. Per shoot: mode (worn / held or used / product only), framing, background, pose style, aspect ratio, views. QA loop always: side-by-side compare, redo failures, max 3 rounds. Hands off to shopify-catalog-upload when asked.

## 2. shopify-catalog-upload
Upload finished catalog photos from a folder to Shopify as products (or as new colour variants of existing products), with titles and descriptions written from the photos. Setup: SKU format (detect from store or define), charge taxes, continue selling when out of stock, sales channels to publish to, copy language (English default, Arabic optional, both via Translate & Adapt), title style, default vendor, publish live or keep as draft. Fixed best practice: build as draft, verify, then go live; link Color to the shopify.color-pattern metafield so swatches show; confirm the store and the full plan before any write.

## 3. cod-rto-analysis
Find where COD orders fail. Groups orders by governorate/city (normalised), courier, product, order value band, customer history, day of week, and payment method; computes delivery success rate, RTO rate, refusal reasons, and money lost to RTO (shipping both ways + COD fee + packaging, from setup or courier CSV). Sources: Shopify orders (connector or export) and optional courier CSVs (any courier, map columns). Output: the worst areas and segments, the cost, and concrete actions (require prepayment or deposit in area X, confirm orders above Y, switch courier in Z), with an estimate of money saved. Setup: currency, courier list, default cost per failed delivery if no courier data, the definition of a failed order in their store (tags, statuses, cancellation reasons), output format.

## 4. weekly-kpi-digest
A weekly one-page snapshot of the business: revenue, orders, AOV, conversion rate (if analytics available), new vs returning customers, top and bottom products, COD share and delivery success, refunds/returns, inventory warnings, vs last week and same week last month, with 3 things to act on. Setup: which KPIs, targets, timezone, week start day, output format, delivery (file, chat, email draft). Scheduled weekly where the agent supports scheduled tasks (Claude scheduled tasks, ChatGPT tasks); otherwise explain how to rerun it. Sources: Shopify connector (orders, analytics via ShopifyQL if available) or exports.

## 5. size-chart-builder
Build accurate size charts for products: from supplier spec sheets, measurements the merchant types, photos of tape-measured garments, or a standard base (EU/US/UK/Egyptian market sizing) adjusted by fit. Output: cm and inches tables, how-to-measure guide, fit notes, in the chosen language(s), as HTML for the product page, an image, or a metafield/page in Shopify (only on confirmation). Setup: unit default, languages, size system, garment categories, brand fit (runs small/true/large), where charts live in the store (page, product description, metafield, app). Flags inconsistencies (sizes not increasing, gaps).

## 6. returns-reason-analysis
Find why customers return or exchange, and what to fix. Reads returns/refunds from Shopify (return reasons, refund line items, order notes, tags) and any returns CSV or support-chat export; classifies free-text reasons into a taxonomy (size too small, too large, quality, not as pictured, wrong item, damaged, changed mind, late delivery), per product, variant, size, colour, supplier; computes return rate and money lost. Output: top products to fix with the specific fix (size chart correction, photo/description change, supplier quality issue), plus trend. Setup: return window, what counts as a return vs exchange, reason taxonomy adjustments, output format.

## 7. demand-planner
Watch stock and sales, predict demand per variant, and say when to reorder and how much: minimise stockouts while keeping cash tied in stock low. Method: daily sales velocity with recent weighting and seasonality (Ramadan, Eid, White Friday) where history allows, demand variability, supplier lead time, safety stock for the chosen service level, reorder point, reorder quantity (economic order quantity or days of cover, respecting MOQ and case packs), stockout dates. Output: reorder list (what, when, how much, cash needed), at-risk items, and cash tied in stock. Setup: lead time per supplier/product (default), service level target, MOQ, case pack, review cycle, unit costs (from Shopify cost per item or a sheet), output format. Dashboard has inputs to change lead time and service level.

## 8. conversion-optimiser
Audit the Shopify storefront for conversion problems, then fix them with the merchant's OK. Audit: speed (Lighthouse/PageSpeed if available), mobile layout at 390px, product page essentials (photos, size chart, delivery time and COD info, returns policy, reviews, sticky add to cart), trust signals, checkout friction, collection navigation and search, Arabic/RTL quality if bilingual, broken links, missing alt text, SEO basics, WhatsApp contact. Output: prioritised fix list (impact x effort). Then acts: on a duplicate theme (never the live one), content edits via the connector, each batch confirmed; preview link before publish. Setup: store URL, theme access method (Shopify CLI, connector, or manual instructions), languages, priorities, what it may change.

## 9. true-profit
Real profit per product, per order and per channel after every cost. Ingests Shopify data (connector or export), Meta/TikTok/Google Ads exports, courier CSVs (shipping, COD fees, RTO charges), and manufacturer/supplier invoices (PDF, image, or sheet) for unit cost. Allocates ad spend (by campaign product mapping, else by revenue share, stated), RTO cost, returns, payment fees, packaging, discounts. Output: profit per product (and which bestsellers lose money), contribution margin, break-even ROAS per product, and actions. Setup: cost sources, payment gateway fees, packaging cost, fixed monthly costs (optional), ad allocation method, currency, output format.

## 10. dead-stock-clearance
Find slow-moving and dead stock and the cash stuck in it, then plan how to clear it. Classifies inventory by days of cover and last sale date, ageing, sell-through; proposes per item: markdown (with suggested price, never below cost unless the merchant allows), bundle with a fast mover (using co-purchase data), free gift threshold, move to a clearance collection, or liquidate. Shows cash recovered and margin impact. No action is taken in the store unless the merchant confirms the exact list (price changes, compare-at prices, collections, discounts). Setup: what counts as slow and dead (days), minimum margin floor, allowed tactics, output format.
