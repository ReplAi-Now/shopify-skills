# Operations

Skills for the numbers behind the store: profit, deliveries, returns and stock. Each one works from the Shopify connector or from your Shopify CSV exports.

- **[true-profit](./true-profit/SKILL.md)**: Real profit per product and order after ads, shipping, COD fees, failed deliveries and returns.
- **[cod-rto-analysis](./cod-rto-analysis/SKILL.md)**: Find where cash on delivery orders fail (governorate, courier, product, customer) and what it costs.
- **[returns-reason-analysis](./returns-reason-analysis/SKILL.md)**: Find why customers return or exchange (English, Arabic and Franco text) and which products to fix.
- **[demand-planner](./demand-planner/SKILL.md)**: When to reorder each variant and how much, balancing stockouts against cash tied up in stock.
- **[dead-stock-clearance](./dead-stock-clearance/SKILL.md)**: Find slow and dead stock, the cash stuck in it, and a clearance plan you approve.
- **[weekly-kpi-digest](./weekly-kpi-digest/SKILL.md)**: A one-page weekly snapshot of the store with three things to act on, on a schedule.
