# Store

Skills for your storefront and catalog.

- **[product-descriptions](./product-descriptions/SKILL.md)**: Write or rewrite product descriptions that sell, plus the SEO title and meta description.
- **[catalog-photoshoot](./catalog-photoshoot/SKILL.md)**: Turn mannequin, flat lay or raw product photos into consistent catalog photos with an AI model, with an automatic quality check.
- **[shopify-catalog-upload](./shopify-catalog-upload/SKILL.md)**: Upload finished photos to Shopify as complete listings (variants, SKUs, swatches, copy), or add new colours to existing products.
- **[size-chart-builder](./size-chart-builder/SKILL.md)**: Build accurate size charts in English or Arabic from supplier sheets or measurements, ready for the product page.
- **[conversion-optimiser](./conversion-optimiser/SKILL.md)**: Audit your store for what stops people buying, then fix it on a theme copy with your OK.
