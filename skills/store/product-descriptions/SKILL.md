# Product Descriptions

Write a product description a shopper takes in at a glance, which answers the questions they would otherwise email about, plus the SEO fields. Every fact in it comes from the user.

## 1. Get the facts

Take what the user gave you (details, an old description, a product page, photos). Then ask once, in one message, for whatever is missing from this list:

- **What it is**, and what's in the box.
- **The specs shoppers check**: size or dimensions, materials, capacity, compatibility, care.
- **Who buys it and why**: the job it does for them.
- **Voice**: two or three of the store's existing descriptions to match, or a few words ("playful", "no-nonsense").

Any spec you still lack goes in a square-bracket placeholder such as `[WIDTH]`. A photo shows colour and shape but not size or material, so ask for those.

Done when: you have the facts, or each gap is a placeholder.

## 2. Write the description

Shape:

1. **Opening line**: what the shopper gets, in their words. It's the headline most shoppers read, so it carries the sale. Spend it on the benefit, since the product title already sits right above it.
2. **3 to 5 bullet points**, each one benefit first, then the fact that proves it: "Keeps drinks cold for 24 hours (double-wall steel)".
3. **The details paragraph**: the practical answers people email about. Size and fit, care, what's in the box, and any delivery or personalisation notes the user mentioned. Every question this paragraph answers is one less support email.

Style:

- 80 to 150 words for most products. Go longer only for expensive or technical products, where shoppers compare specs.
- Concrete over grand: "holds a 15-inch laptop" beats "spacious". Every adjective is backed by a fact.
- Second person ("you"), present tense, and the store's spelling (UK or US).
- Match the voice examples when given; otherwise plain and warm.
- Leave the price out. Shopify shows it beside the description.

Where a claim is regulated (health, supplements, cosmetics, food, children's products), use only the claims the user supplied, word for word, and flag them in the notes.

## 3. Write the SEO fields

These go in the **Search engine listing** section at the bottom of Shopify's product editor.

- **Page title**: under 60 characters, so Google shows it in full. The product type plus the main thing that sets it apart, then ` | [STORE NAME]` if it fits.
- **Meta description**: under 155 characters. The two or three facts a searcher compares on, as a plain sentence.

Done when: you have counted the characters in both fields, both are under their limits, and every fact in them also appears in the description.

## 4. Hand it over

Give, in this order: the description, the page title, the meta description, then a short **Before you publish** list of placeholders to fill and claims to check.

Format the description as plain text with bullet points, which pastes cleanly into Shopify's description box. If the user asks for HTML (for the code view or a CSV import), use only `<p>`, `<ul>`, `<li>` and `<strong>`.

For several products, write the first one and ask whether the voice and length are right. Then write the rest in the same shape.

## Example

**User**: Ceramic pour-over coffee dripper. Makes 1 to 4 cups. Takes size 02 paper filters. Dishwasher safe. Handmade in Portugal, so the glaze varies. £34. Our customers are people getting into specialty coffee.

**Description**:

> Make café-style pour-over coffee at home, with a dripper made by hand in Portugal.
>
> - Makes 1 to 4 cups, so it suits a quiet morning or a pot for friends
> - Takes standard size 02 paper filters
> - Dishwasher safe, so cleaning up is easy
> - Handmade, so every glaze is a little different
>
> Because each dripper is glazed by hand, yours won't match the photo exactly. It measures [BASE DIAMETER] across the base, so check it sits on your mug or carafe before you buy.

**Page title**: Handmade Ceramic Pour-Over Coffee Dripper (41 characters)

**Meta description**: Handmade in Portugal. Brews 1 to 4 cups, takes size 02 filters and goes in the dishwasher. Every glaze is one of a kind. (120 characters)

**Before you publish**:

- Fill in `[BASE DIAMETER]`.
