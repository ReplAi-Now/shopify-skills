# Pose library

Every image in a batch gets a different pose, including the front and back of the same product. Rotate the starting pose between batches. If the batch is bigger than the list, write a new pose in the same spirit rather than repeating one. The template already adds: legs close together, no hands on hips, product never covered.

Modest and hijab setups: use the poses marked (M) only, or any pose where arms stay low and close to the body. Kids: calm, natural standing poses only (F1, F4, F5, B1, B4).

## Worn: front
- F1 (M) Classic: standing upright, feet almost touching, weight slightly on one leg, arms hanging loosely along the sides, shoulders relaxed.
- F2 (M) Contrapposto: weight on the right leg, left knee softly bent forward, right hand lightly resting on the side of the thigh, left arm relaxed.
- F3 Hair touch: one hand gently tucking hair behind the ear, the other arm relaxed, head slightly tilted.
- F4 (M) Soft turn: body turned about 20 degrees to the left, face and gaze toward the camera, feet together, arms relaxed.
- F5 (M) Walking: caught mid-step walking toward the camera, one foot slightly in front of the other, arms swinging naturally close to the body.
- F6 (M) Holding the fabric: standing with ankles lightly crossed, one hand delicately holding a bit of the skirt or coat edge at the side.
- F7 Knee in: one knee bent slightly inward, both hands resting lightly on the sides of the thighs.
- F8 (M) Looking away: weight on one leg, face turned slightly to the side, soft gaze away from the camera, arms loose.
- F9 Collarbone: fingertips lightly touching the side of the neck without covering the neckline, the other arm relaxed.
- F10 Heel lifted: standing on one leg with the other heel slightly lifted and knee bent forward, arms relaxed, gentle smile.
- F11 (M) Hands clasped: hands loosely clasped in front at hip height, below any waist details, shoulders relaxed.
- F12 (M) Bag carry (bags): bag on the shoulder or held by the handle at the side, arm relaxed, bag fully visible.

## Worn: back
- B1 (M) Classic back: seen from behind, feet almost touching, weight on one leg, arms hanging loosely.
- B2 (M) Over the shoulder: seen from behind, head turned to look back over the right shoulder in profile, arms relaxed.
- B3 Hair up: seen from behind, both hands gathering the hair above the head so the full back is visible. Not for tops with complex sleeves.
- B4 (M) Walking away: mid-step walking away from the camera, arms swinging naturally.
- B5 (M) Back three-quarter: seen from behind with the body turned about 20 degrees, one hand lightly on the side of the thigh.
- B6 Hair over one shoulder: seen from behind, hair swept forward over one shoulder so the back is fully visible.
- B7 (M) Heel lifted back: seen from behind, standing on one leg with the other heel slightly lifted, arms relaxed.

## Worn: shoes (framed knees down)
- S1 Standing straight, feet parallel and slightly apart, toes to camera.
- S2 One foot forward and turned out 30 degrees to show the side profile.
- S3 Mid-step walking, back heel lifted, sole partly visible.
- S4 Seated on a low white block, legs together, shoes angled toward the camera.

## Worn: jewellery (close-up)
- J1 Necklace: head slightly turned, chin lifted, hair or hijab clear of the neckline.
- J2 Earrings: profile view, hair tucked behind the ear (or hijab framing the ear area if the merchant wants earrings visible over it).
- J3 Bracelet or watch: forearm resting naturally, wrist turned to show the face of the piece.
- J4 Ring: hand relaxed on the collarbone or on the opposite forearm, fingers softly separated.

## Held / used
- H1 Holding the product at chest height in one hand, label to camera, soft smile.
- H2 Two hands presenting the product slightly forward, elbows close to the body.
- H3 Applying or using it naturally (lipstick near the lips, cream on the back of the hand, phone in hand with the case visible), product label still visible.
- H4 Product resting in an open palm at waist height, the other hand relaxed.
- H5 Looking at the product with a soft smile, product angled three-quarter to camera.

## Product only
- P1 Front, straight on, centered.
- P2 Three-quarter view from slightly above.
- P3 Top-down flat view (flat items, sets, kits).
- P4 Side profile (shoes, bottles with a shaped profile).
- P5 Detail macro: texture, stitching, hardware or stone setting.
- P6 Group shot: all colours or the set arranged in a neat row or fan.
